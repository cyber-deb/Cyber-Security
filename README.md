The mindset I want to develop is:

```text
Observe
   ↓
Enumerate
   ↓
Understand
   ↓
Form a Hypothesis
   ↓
Test
   ↓
Exploit / Validate
   ↓
Enumerate Again
   ↓
Escalate
   ↓
Document
   ↓
Remediate
```

The goal is to understand **why** something works rather than memorizing commands.

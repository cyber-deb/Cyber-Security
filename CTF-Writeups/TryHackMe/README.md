# 📝 Writeup Standard

Each substantial CTF will have its own directory or Markdown writeup.

A typical writeup follows:

```text
Challenge
│
├── Overview
├── Objective
├── Reconnaissance
├── Enumeration
├── Initial Access
├── Exploitation
├── Privilege Escalation
├── Flags / Findings
├── Tools Used
├── What I Learned
├── Personal Reflection
└── Conclusion
```

Not every challenge requires every section.

The writeup should explain **the reasoning behind the steps**, not just list commands.

# 🧠 CTF Methodology

My goal is to approach challenges systematically rather than randomly trying commands.

```text
Recon
 ↓
Enumeration
 ↓
Identify Attack Surface
 ↓
Find Clues
 ↓
Form Hypothesis
 ↓
Test
 ↓
Initial Access
 ↓
Enumerate Again
 ↓
Privilege Escalation
 ↓
Objective
 ↓
Document
```

When stuck, I want to ask:

1. What do I know?
2. What don't I know?
3. What information can I gather?
4. What assumptions am I making?
5. What can I test safely?
6. What did the result teach me?

A CTF is not just:

It is an opportunity to practice:

> **Observe → Enumerate → Understand → Hypothesize → Test → Adapt → Solve**

The most valuable part of a challenge is understanding **why the attack worked**.
